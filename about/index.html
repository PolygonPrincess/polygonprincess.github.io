Who am I?

Hi! I'm Polygon Princess, but many people call me Poly! I am a non-binary trans woman in my 30s and my pronouns are She/They. As you can see, I create content on Twitch, make music for myself and others, and program! Aside from creating content and music, I've been building desktop computers for the better part of 10 years!

I cherish my friends and family dearly. Fun fact about my family: I didn't know half of my family until the beginning of the COVID-19 pandemic! We actually met through an online AncestryDNA test. You can imagine how shocking that was for all of us involved. My story was featured on Ancestry's page in a video during Pride month, and if you'd like to take a look, here's a link to that video!

Content Creator Journey

My content creation journey technically started on Twitch in 2018, but it wasn't until late 2019/early 2020 that I really got going. I've gone through a variety of games over the years, be it Overwatch, Dead by Daylight, Super Mario Maker, Minecraft, amongst many others. I simply play the things that bring me joy and share that joy with others. Apart from video games, I've done a few streams dabbling in makeup, drag, creating wigs, tier lists of RuPaul's Drag Race contestants who have escaped the matrix (that was fun), and even streamed with my dad!

Throughout my Twitch journey, I've been fortunate enough to raise money independently as well as participate in and lead group efforts for various charitable causes. As a result, I've met quite a few lovely people along the way! I am a Team Lead for Team Overture, a stream team of diverse creators looking to create cool shit. I am also part of both the Twitch Pride Guild and Twitch Women's Guild! I feel so lucky to have the opportunity to collaborate with talented and passionate creators through these communities!

Music Artistry

My musical journey begins all the way in fifth grade (not clickbait)! I played trumpet for a year and took a piano class in high school. That's also when I discovered FL Studio, the digital audio workstation I use to this day! I've always been interested in music, be it as a listener or creator. Music to me is a universal language and a very versatile art form. I've been casually producing since 2009, although from 2022 onward I've been slowly pushing myself to take it more seriously!

On the homepage you'll find a playlist of snippets and things I've uploaded to SoundCloud over the years. However, that's not everything! I don't just create music for myself, but for others as well, be it as a producer, songwriter, mixer, or vocalist! I've had a few collaborations with artists such as Sum Ting Wong and I've produced a few mixes for performers such as PearlTeese including the opening for the 2025 TwitchCon Drag Showcase! My Spotify and Apple Music profiles are on the home page and trust me, there's more music to come! ;)
